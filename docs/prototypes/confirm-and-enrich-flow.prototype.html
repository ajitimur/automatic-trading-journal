The confirm-and-enrich capture flow

Nothing in this journal commits blindly. A broker statement is dropped in, parsed, reconciled against what is already journalled, and offered back as proposals a human confirms or corrects. Only then does anything become a Trade, and only then does enrichment run. This page is that flow made concrete so you can push it through the cases that are hard to argue about on paper — a partial exit against two open Trades in the same symbol, a broker restating a fill after the Trade froze, a statement whose columns shifted, an exit for an entry that was never journalled.

The question this prototype exists to answer:
  • Is the confirm queue the right single door — for broker imports and hand-entered backdated Trades?
  • Does treating every failure as a queue item with a defined behaviour (rather than an error) actually cover the cases?
  • Is "correct a fact once, remember a rule forever" the right line for not re-confirming the same misparse every week?
  • Settled: stop and setup are chaseable, never demanded at confirm — so a Trade can commit with risk % held open, and freezing without a stop makes that hole permanent. Does the nag land in the right place?
  • Does splitting enrichment into entry-dated at confirm and rolling in the daily job hold up?

Prices, fees, fill structures and the Stockbit fee identity are the real ones from docs/samples/. Bars, equity and the calendar are faked. No persistence — reload to reset.
