Stockbit Statement of Account — EQUITY-BEARING SECTIONS, STRUCTURE ONLY
=======================================================================

Extracted with `pdftotext -layout` from the same real monthly SoA (July 2026)
already characterised in `stockbit-soa-structure.txt`. That file covers page 1's
cash ledger. This file covers what issue #19 went looking for: the header
summary box and the PORTFOLIO STATEMENT.

EVERY MONETARY FIGURE IS SCRUBBED. This repository is public and these are real
account balances. Quantities and prices are scrubbed too — unlike the ledger
extract, here they multiply straight back into a portfolio value. Symbols are
replaced with SYM1..SYM6. Only the LAYOUT and the ARITHMETIC IDENTITIES are
real, and the identities were verified to the rupiah against the raw document.

2. PORTFOLIO STATEMENT (last page, after the ledger)
-----------------------------------------------------

Column header, verbatim (it spans two physical header lines, with `PRICE`
straddling Buying/Close and `UNREAL. GAIN/LOSS` straddling the last two):

                                                    PRICE                                       UNREAL. GAIN/LOSS
    Stocks        Special Notes  Margin  Quantity   Buying   Close   Buying Value  Market Value  (Rp.)      %

Representative rows (all figures scrubbed):

    SYM1  <Company Name Tbk.>                <QTY>  <PRICE>  <PRICE>  <AMT>  <AMT>  <AMT>   <PCT>
    SYM2  <Company Name                      <QTY>  <PRICE>  <PRICE>  <AMT>  <AMT>  <AMT>   <PCT>
          Tbk.>
    ...
                                             TOTAL                          <AMT>  <AMT>  <AMT>

Layout facts a parser must survive:

  - The `Stocks ... %` header line REPEATS partway down the table. It is not a
    page break in the text layer — `pdftotext -layout` emits it mid-run. Do not
    treat a header line as a table terminator.
  - Long company names WRAP onto a second line carrying no figures. A row is
    identified by its leading ticker, not by line count.
  - `Special Notes` and `Margin` were EMPTY on every row in this sample, so
    their column positions are unconfirmed. A name carrying an IDX special
    notation (the A/B/D/E/F/G/I/L/M/N/S/X/Y legend is printed below the table)
    would populate `Special Notes` and could shift the layout.
  - The section sits after the ledger, on the last page. Page count varies with
    the month's activity, so FIND THE `PORTFOLIO STATEMENT` HEADING — never
    index a fixed page.


3. IDENTITIES — verified exactly, to the rupiah
------------------------------------------------

These are what make the document self-checking, in the same way the daily Trade
Confirmation's fee model does (see export-findings.md).

  (a) per row:   Market Value = Quantity x Close
                 Verified on all 6 holdings, exact, no rounding slack.

  (b) column:    sum(Market Value over rows) = printed TOTAL Market Value
                 Verified exact.

  (c) box:       Portfolio  = TOTAL Market Value from the table
                 Verified exact.

  (d) equity:    Equity NAB = Portfolio + <ledger closing Ending Balance>
                 Verified exact.

  (e) cash:      Cash       = Cash Investor + <ledger closing Ending Balance>
                 Avail Limit = Cash
                 Verified to WITHIN ONE RUPIAH, not exact. `Cash Investor` is
                 the only figure in the box printed with 2 decimals; the ledger
                 balance is printed rounded, so the sum lands under a rupiah
                 away from the printed `Cash`. Identity (d) IS exact as
                 printed. Do not build a hard equality assertion on (e).

The consequence of (d) and (e) together is the important one, and it is a
DECISION, not a fact — see docs/samples/equity-sources-findings.md:

    Equity NAB counts the unsettled trading balance but NOT `Cash Investor`.
    So `Equity NAB` and `Portfolio + Cash` are two DIFFERENT printed candidate
    denominators, and on this sample they differ by about a fifth of the
    larger. Which one is "account equity" for risk % is issue #16's call.

The ledger's closing Ending Balance is printed in parentheses. Parentheses are
the broker's sign convention for a balance owed TO the client: on this sample
`Cr - Db` equals the parenthesised figure exactly, and it enters (d) and (e)
POSITIVE. Do not read the parentheses as a negative client balance.


4. DIVIDENDS APPEAR IN THE LEDGER
----------------------------------

Not what #19 asked, but it corrects a premise carried in #17. The cash ledger
carries dividend rows, as a matched pair (accrual then payment):

    31/07/2026  31/07/2026  D <REF> Dividend SYM <QTY> @ <RATE> - Tax 0%   ...
    31/07/2026  31/07/2026  P <REF> Dividend SYM <QTY> @ <RATE> - Tax 0%   ...

Share count, per-share rate and the withholding rate are all printed. So IDX
dividend cash IS reachable — in the MONTHLY SoA, not the daily TC.
