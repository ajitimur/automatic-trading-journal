Row grammar for the trade sub-lines:

  <reference> <B|S>: <board> <symbol> <quantity> @ <price>=<net amount>

  B / S     buy or sell
  RG        IDX board code (Reguler)
  quantity  SHARES, always a multiple of 100 (not lots)
  price     may carry up to 4 decimals — a weighted average, not a tick price
  net       gross +/- fees, already baked in; fees are never itemised
